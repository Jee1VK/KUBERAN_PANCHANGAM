Kuberan Panchangam — Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.kuberansilks.panchangam • Category: Lifestyle / Books & Reference / Astrology

1. 100% On-Device Computation (Zero Server Transmission)

Kuberan Panchangam performs all Vedic astronomical calculations—including Daily Panchangam (Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Rahu Kala), Janma Jathaka / Kundli, D9 Navamsha, Vimshottari Dasha, and Ashtakoota Marriage Matching—strictly inside your device memory using offline VSOP87 / Swiss Ephemeris analytical mechanics.

  • Jathaka & Kundli Inputs: Personal details you enter (Name, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, Place Coordinates) remain exclusively on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, logged, or shared with Kuberan Silks servers or any third party.
  • No Accounts, No Ads, No Tracking: The app requires no sign-up or login and contains zero advertising SDKs, zero third-party analytics, and zero behavioral tracking cookies.

2. Location Permission & Optional Network Requests

  • GPS Location (Optional): When you tap auto-detect location, your coordinates are used ephemerally on-device to calculate exact local Sunrise, Sunset, Tithi end-times, and Lagna for your city. You may decline GPS permission and use the built-in 100% offline city database at any time.
  • Reverse Geocoding (Optional): Only if you explicitly trigger GPS detection or online city search while connected, a latitude/longitude or search query is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to resolve a city name. Astrological calculations work 100% offline.

3. Local Storage Sovereignty & Clear-History Control (DPDP Act 2023)

In full alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the EU GDPR, recent Jathaka history, saved Family Profiles, selected Moon sign (Rashi), Ayanamsa choice, and language preference are stored solely inside your device's local storage (localStorage / WebView defaults). Recent Jathaka history auto-deletes after 48 hours.

Self-Service Data Erasure: Tap the button below to permanently erase all Jathaka history, saved Family Profiles, Moon sign selection, and app preferences from this device immediately.
