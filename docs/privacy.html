1. 100% On-Device Computation & AES-256-GCM Encryption at Rest

Kuberan Panchangam performs all Vedic astronomical calculations—including Daily Panchangam (Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Rahu Kala), Janma Jathaka / Kundli, D9 Navamsha, Vimshottari Dasha, and Ashtakoota Marriage Matching—strictly inside your device memory using offline VSOP87 / Swiss Ephemeris analytical mechanics.

  • Encrypted Local Vault: Personal birth details saved in Family Profiles (Name, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, Place Coordinates) are encrypted at rest on your device using Web Crypto AES-256-GCM with a device-local 256-bit random key. They are never uploaded, transmitted, logged, or shared with Kuberan Silks servers or any third party.
  • OS Keyboard & Backup Hardening: Sensitive birth inputs disable OS predictive keyboard learning (autocomplete="off", autocorrect="off", spellcheck="false"), and Android cloud auto-backup is disabled (allowBackup="false") so local WebView storage is never silently copied to cloud drives.
  • No Accounts, No Ads, No Tracking: The app requires no sign-up or login and contains zero advertising SDKs, zero third-party analytics, and zero behavioral tracking cookies.

3. Explicit Retention Schedule, Data Portability & Erasure (DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025)

In full alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the DPDP Rules, 2025, and the EU GDPR, strict storage limitation and self-service Data Principal controls are enforced on-device:

  • Recent Jathaka History (kuberan_jathaka_history): Automatically purged on-device after 48 hours.
  • Saved Family Profiles (kuberan_panchangam_family_profiles_v1): Encrypted at rest via AES-256-GCM and retained locally on your device solely until you delete individual profiles or trigger the complete Purge below.

4. Children's Privacy (Under 18 Years — DPDP Act, 2023 Section 9)

Under Section 9 of India's DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, a child is defined as any individual under 18 years of age. Kuberan Panchangam is designed for general adult cultural and astronomical reference (18+). We do not knowingly collect or transmit personal data from children under 18 to any server, and the app performs zero behavioral monitoring, profiling, tracking, or targeted advertising directed at minors. Any family birth chart entered on a household device remains strictly encrypted on that local device under parental/adult control and can be erased immediately above.
