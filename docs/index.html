📖 Vedic Masa, Adhikamasa & Kshaya Tithi Guide
1. Amanta Lunar Masa (ಅಮಾವಾಸ್ಯಾಂತ ಮಾಸ):
Followed across Karnataka & South India. Each lunar month begins on Shukla Prathama (day after New Moon) and concludes on Amavasya. It is named after the solar Rashi into which the Sun transits during that lunation.
2. Adhika Masa / Purushottama Masa (ಅಧಿಕ ಮಾಸ):
Occurs every ~32.5 months when two Amavasyas (New Moons) fall within a single Solar Rashi without a Sankranti in between. Sacred for Japa, Parayana, and Dana; routine weddings and Griha Pravesha are deferred to the Nija Masa.
3. Kshaya & Vriddhi Tithi (ಕ್ಷಯ ಮತ್ತು ವೃದ್ಧಿ ತಿಥಿ):
A Kshaya Tithi begins after one sunrise and ends before the next sunrise (touching 0 sunrises). A Vriddhi Tithi touches two consecutive sunrises. Our Drik engine evaluates exact Kaala Vyapti (Udaya, Madhyahna, Pradosha, Nishita, Arunodaya, and Aparahna for Shraddha).
